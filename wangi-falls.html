Terms of Use — Guest Wi-Fi

1. Service Overview

The NT Parks & Wildlife Guest Wi-Fi ("Network") at Wangi Falls is provided by the Parks and Wildlife Commission of the Northern Territory for park visitors. By connecting you agree to these Terms of Use.

2. Permitted Use

This Network is for visitor use only. Permitted activities include accessing park information, maps and fact sheets; making bookings or purchasing a Parks Pass; checking road and closure conditions; and general web browsing.

You must not use this Network to engage in unlawful, harmful, or fraudulent activity; download or share illegal content; conduct large file transfers or peer-to-peer sharing; or attempt to access or disrupt the Network or other users.

This is a public Wi-Fi network. Connect at your own risk. We recommend using a VPN. Do not transmit sensitive personal or financial information on this Network.
