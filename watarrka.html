Welcome to Kings Canyon Watarrka National Park

Before connecting, please note that Wi-Fi access here is limited and may be slow due to our remote location. Mobile phone coverage is not guaranteed, so make sure you have enough water, sun protection, and tell someone your plans before exploring. We hope you enjoy your visit and stay safe.

NT Parks Visitor Pass

If you’re a visitor to the NT, you need a NT Parks Visitor Pass to visit most national parks and reserves, including Watarrka National Park.

The visitor pass allows you to visit all parks and reserves managed by the NT Government within your valid dates.

You must connect to the Wi-Fi, then access the NT parks booking system to get a visitor pass.

NT residents don’t need a visitor pass but may be asked to show proof of residency, such as a valid NT driver licence.

Watarrka walk and hike information

Rim walk

You must walk this trail in a clockwise direction. The trail is marked with blue arrows.

Distance 6km loop
Time 3 to 4 hours
Grade Moderate to difficult

The rim walk starts with a difficult climb so you should only walk it if you are fit and healthy.

The walk ascends to the top of the canyon and follows the rim around before descending to the car park.

About halfway along is the Garden of Eden, a beautiful rockhole surrounded by rare plants. There is no swimming in the waterhole.

Be careful during the hotter months, from September to April. During this time, you should consider walking other shorter walks during the middle of the day.

On days when the temperature forecast is 36°C or above, you must start the Kings Canyon Rim Walk before 9am. You will not be allowed to start the walk after 9am.

Stay on the stairs and boardwalks along the track. They are there to make your walk safe and protect the environment.

Kings Creek walk

This walk is marked with red markers.

Distance 2.6km return
Time Around 1 hour
Grade Easy

This walk is suitable for families and people seeking an easy walk.

Follow the same track to return to the carpark.

You must have sturdy shoes for this trail.

South wall return walk

This walk is marked with green arrows to the gate and blue arrows back.

Distance 4.8km return
Time Around 2 hours
Grade Moderate

The walk starts with a steep climb to the south wall of Kings Canyon leading to stunning views.

The walk ends at a one-way gate. You will return to the car park by the same route.

On days when the temperature forecast is 36°C or above, you must start the walk before 11am.

You can’t access the Garden of Eden or the rest of the rim walk from the South Wall return walk.

Kathleen Springs

This walk is a sealed track without markers.

Distance 2.4km return
Time Around 1.5 hours
Grade Easy

This walk is good for families and visitors with limited mobility. There is wheelchair access for this walk.

Signs along the track will tell you stories of Aboriginal culture and the recent cattle industry.

The trail leads to a spring-fed waterhole at the head of Kathleen Gorge. This is a cool place to sit and enjoy the area’s tranquillity.

Heat management strategy

The Parks and Wildlife Commission has introduced a strategy to improve visitor safety at Watarrka. On days when the temperature is forecast to be 36°C or above*, visitors wishing to undertake the Rim Walk need to start their walk before 9am.

On days when the temperature is forecast to be 36°C or above*, visitors wishing to undertake the South Wall Return Walk need to start their walk before 11am.

Signs will be updated daily to indicate current access conditions. There is no drinking water available on the Rim.

* Based on Bureau of Meteorology forecasts for Watarrka
Safety information

You can have a safe and comfortable trip to Watarrka National Park by doing all of the following:

  • observe park safety signs
  • stay at least 2m from cliff edges
  • carry and drink plenty of water
  • wear a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, suitable clothing and footwear
  • avoid strenuous activity during the heat of the day
  • think about your health and fitness when choosing a walk
  • download the Avenza Maps app for free, interactive park maps on your mobile phone or tablet (you must connect to the Wi-Fi first).

Accommodation

Overnight camping in tents, caravans or motorhomes is not allowed in the National Park.

Commercial accommodation, camping and bunkhouses are available at Kings Canyon Resort.

Camping and safari-style cabins are also available at Kings Creek Station.

In an emergency

Emergency Call Devices (ECDs) are located on the Kings Canyon Rim Walk and at the Canyon car park. Please use them to contact the Rangers in an emergency only.
