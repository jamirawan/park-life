Terms of Use — Guest Wi-Fi

1. Service Overview

The NT Parks & Wildlife Guest Wi-Fi ("Network") at Watarrka National Park is provided by the Parks and Wildlife Commission of the Northern Territory for park visitors. By connecting you agree to these Terms of Use.

2. Permitted Use

This Network is for visitor use only. Permitted activities include accessing park information, trail maps and fact sheets; making campsite bookings or purchasing a Parks Pass; checking road and trail conditions; and general web browsing.

3. Prohibited Activities

You must not use this Network to engage in unlawful, harmful or fraudulent activity; download or share illegal content; conduct large file transfers or peer-to-peer sharing; or attempt to access or disrupt the Network or other users.

Connection activity may be logged for network management and analytics in accordance with NT and Commonwealth legislation. By connecting you consent to this collection.
