Terms of Use — Guest Wi-Fi

1. Service Overview

The NT Parks & Wildlife Guest Wi-Fi network ("Network") is provided by the Parks and Wildlife Commission of the Northern Territory for the use of park visitors accessing the Network at designated sites including Wangi Falls, Karlu Karlu and Watarrka National Park. By connecting to this Network you agree to these Terms of Use.

2. Permitted Use

This Network is provided to support your visit to NT Parks. Permitted uses include:

  • Accessing park information, maps, and fact sheets
  • Making campsite bookings or purchasing a Parks Pass
  • Checking road and closure conditions
  • General web browsing and communication
  • Accessing emergency contacts and safety information

3. Prohibited Activities

You must not use this Network to:

  • Engage in any unlawful, harmful, or fraudulent activity
  • Download, upload, or distribute illegal or offensive content
  • Conduct large file transfers or peer-to-peer sharing that degrades the Network
  • Attempt to access, monitor, or disrupt other users or network infrastructure
  • Engage in commercial activity or unsolicited communications

4. Availability & Liability

The Network is provided on an "as is" basis. The Parks and Wildlife Commission does not guarantee availability, speed, reliability, or security of the service. We reserve the right to restrict or terminate access at any time. The Commission accepts no liability for loss or damage resulting from use of this Network.

5. Privacy & Data Collection

Your connection activity may be logged for network management, security, and analytics purposes in accordance with applicable Northern Territory and Commonwealth legislation. De-identified usage data may be used to improve park services. By connecting, you consent to this data collection.

6. Security

This is a public Wi-Fi network. You connect at your own risk. We recommend using a VPN and keeping your device security up to date. Do not transmit sensitive personal or financial information on this Network.

These Terms of Use are governed by the laws of the Northern Territory of Australia. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the Northern Territory.
